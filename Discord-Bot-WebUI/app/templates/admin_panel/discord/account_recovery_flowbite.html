    {# ============ What this page is for ============ #}

    Someone tried to log in with a Discord account we didn't recognise as theirs.

    Almost always this is a member who lost access to their old Discord and made a new one. We refuse the login on purpose — it looks identical to someone trying to take over an account — so a human confirms it. Approving moves the new Discord ID onto their existing player, keeps all their history, and syncs their roles. They then log in normally with the new Discord.

    Before approving, confirm out-of-band that it's really them — a Discord DM or a message in a channel you know they control.
